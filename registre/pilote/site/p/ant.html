What If an Ant Got Twice as Strong Every Second?

An ant gets twice as strong every second: it lifts an egg at second 10, you at second 20, the Statue of Liberty at second 32 and Hoover Dam at second 46. A real ant carries about 8.78 times its own weight, not 50. But there are 20 quadrillion of them: 2.4 million for each of us.

Every number is computed, nothing is written by hand. Sources: Segre & Taylor, Journal of Experimental Biology 2019; Schultheiss et al., PNAS 2022; UN World Population Prospects 2024; USDA; PetMD; Walpole et al., BMC Public Health 2012; Hutchinson et al., PLoS ONE 2011; National Park Service; toureiffel.paris; US Navy; US Bureau of Reclamation.
