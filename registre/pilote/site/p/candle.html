What If a Candle Got 4 Times Stronger Every Second?

A candle releases 4 times more energy every second. Stronger than a horse at second 2, a dam at second 13, all of humanity at second 19, a hurricane at second 22, and at second 41 it shines like the Sun. The catch: it would burn a Moon's worth of wax every 2.6 hours, about 1.9 billion times more fuel than the real Sun, which runs on nuclear fusion.

Every number is computed, nothing is written by hand. Sources: Wikipedia (Candle); NIST; US DOE / LBNL; US Bureau of Reclamation; Energy Institute (2024); NOAA AOML; IAU 2015; Ribas et al. (2017); NASA.
