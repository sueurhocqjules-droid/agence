What If Your Eyesight Got 30% Sharper Every Second?

Your eyesight gets 30% sharper every second. At second 4 you see like an eagle, at second 17 you could spot a person from the space station, at second 28 you beat the Hubble telescope, and at second 55 you can see a black hole. The catch: physics says your pupil would then need to be 2.6 miles wide.

Every number is computed, nothing is written by hand. Sources: Webvision (University of Utah); US Mint; Reymond (1985); NASA; FIFA; ESA Hubble; Kervella et al. (2003); ESO; Event Horizon Telescope (2019); OpenStax University Physics.
