What If One Bacterium Doubled Every Second?

One E. coli bacterium weighs about a picogram. What if it split in two every second? It outweighs a red blood cell at second 7, a grain of salt at second 26, a mosquito at second 32, a mouse at second 45, and at second 56 the pile weighs as much as you. Real E. coli takes about 20 minutes per split: at that pace, it would outweigh the Earth in 44 hours. It never does, because the food runs out first.

Every number is computed, nothing is written by hand. Sources: BioNumbers; Walpole et al., BMC Public Health (2012); NASA Earth Fact Sheet; Leif & Vinograd, PNAS (1964); Martínez-Martín et al., Nature (2017); Oregon State University, General Microbiology; American Mosquito Control Association; Britannica.
