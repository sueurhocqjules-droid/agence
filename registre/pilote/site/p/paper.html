What If You Folded Paper Once More Every Second?

Every fold doubles a 0.1 mm sheet: fold 42 passes the Moon, fold 69 reaches the nearest star, about 10x thinner than an atom.

Every number is computed, nothing is written by hand. Sources: ISO 216, NASA, IAU, ESA Gaia, CODATA. Real record: 12 folds (Britney Gallivan, 2002).
