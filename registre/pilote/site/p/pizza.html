What If You Cut a Pizza Into 4 Every Second?

Cut a pizza into 4 every second and keep only one piece. At cut 8 it weighs less than a grain of rice, at cut 25 less than a bacterium, and at cut 43 your piece is a single atom. That's when the knife has to stop: in Greek, "atom" literally means "uncuttable".

Every number is computed, nothing is written by hand. Sources: USDA FoodData Central; BioNumbers (Harvard); Vollmer et al., PNAS (2008); Sigma-Aldrich; CODATA 2018.
