What If a Hydraulic Press Pushed 40% Harder Every Second?

A hydraulic press pushes 40% harder every second on a block of graphite, the stuff in pencils. Espresso pressure at second 7, steel starts to flow at second 24, diamond-making pressure at second 33, and at second 45 it matches the center of the Earth. The twist: labs reach that pressure every day with two tiny diamonds and the force of a 25 lb weight.

Every number is computed, nothing is written by hand. Sources: NIST; Specialty Coffee Association; ASTM A36; GIA Gems & Gemology; Scientific Reports (2018); PREM; Nature (2008); O'Bannon et al. (2018).
