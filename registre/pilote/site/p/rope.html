What If Your Shoelace Doubled in Length Every Second?

A sneaker lace is about 47 inches long. Now double it every second: longer than a bus at second 4, taller than Everest at 13, Paris to London at 19, around the Earth at 25, to the Moon at 29, to the Sun at 37, and at 45 it reaches Voyager 1, which just became the first probe one light-day from Earth. By then light needs 39 hours to cross it, and its tip has been outrunning light since second 29.

Every number is computed, nothing is written by hand. Sources: Kicks Shoelaces, FIFA, CTBUH, China and Nepal (2020), NASA fact sheets and JPL, IAU 2012, BIPM.
