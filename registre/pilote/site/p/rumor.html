What If Everyone Who Heard Your Secret Told 2 New People?

You tell a secret to 2 people, one per second, and everyone who hears it does the same. Your whole class knows at second 6, the biggest cruise ship at 18, New York City at 32, India at 43 and every person on Earth at second 47. Then there's nobody left to tell, except the 4 babies born every second.

Every number is computed, nothing is written by hand. Sources: UN World Population Prospects 2024, UNFPA, U.S. Census Bureau, NYC Department of City Planning, NCES, Wikipedia.
