What If Your Sneeze Got 50% Faster Every Second?

A real sneeze tops out around 10 mph. Now make it 50% faster every second: it outruns a cheetah at second 5, breaks the sound barrier at 11, leaves Earth for good at 20, beats the fastest spacecraft at 27 and reaches the Moon in 1.5 seconds at 44. At second 45 it would have to outrun light. The universe says: bless you.

Every number is computed, nothing is written by hand. Sources: Tang et al. (PLOS ONE, 2013), Wilson et al. (Nature, 2013), Guinness World Records, RUAG, NASA fact sheets, Koposov et al. (MNRAS, 2019), BIPM.
