What If a Snowball Got 60% Heavier Every Second?

A snowball gets 60% heavier every second as it rolls downhill: it outweighs you after 13 seconds, a blue whale at second 29, the Eiffel Tower at second 38 and the Great Pyramid at second 52. But a snowball can only grow by eating snow: by the end it has swallowed 3 feet of snow from 5,300 soccer fields.

Every number is computed, nothing is written by hand. Sources: Paterson, The Physics of Glaciers; USBC; Walpole et al., BMC Public Health 2012; Smithsonian's National Zoo; NOAA Fisheries; NASA; toureiffel.paris; Harland & Wolff records; Guinness World Records; IEEE Spectrum; FIFA.
