What If Water Got 3 Times Thicker Every Second?

Water gets 3 times thicker every second: olive oil at second 4, honey at second 9, pitch (one drop per decade) at second 24, thicker than granite at second 48, and by second 51 it is as thick as Earth's mantle, which still flows under your feet about as fast as your fingernails grow.

Every number is computed, nothing is written by hand. Sources: CRC Handbook of Chemistry and Physics; University of Rhode Island; Edgeworth et al. 1984 (University of Queensland pitch drop); IUPAC; Shibley, Lai & Culberg 2023; Mukherjee, Talbot & Koyi 2010; Kumagai et al. 1978; Fjeldskaar 1994; USGS.
