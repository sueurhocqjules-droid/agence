What If You Zoomed In 2x Every Second?

Start with your hand and zoom in twice as close every second. Your fingerprint at second 3, a skin cell at second 10, a bacterium at second 16, DNA at second 24, a carbon atom at second 28... and then 15 seconds of nothing before the nucleus. If the nucleus were 1 cm wide, the atom would span 2.7 soccer fields: it is 99.9999999999956% empty space.

Every number is computed, nothing is written by hand. Sources: CODATA 2018; Wikipedia (atomic radii, corneocyte); electron-scattering measurements of carbon-12; Viruses (2023) on phage T4; Durham University; MicrobeWiki; FIFA Football Stadiums Guidelines.
